Biography

Joyce (Ruozhou) Wei

Joyce (Ruozhou) Wei (b.2004) creates mixed-media objects that explore the multifaceted complexities of latency and concealment, as if trapped between the tabletop and a glass covering.

Indexical traces gather in layered forms that remain allusive and elusive, often charged with a sense of vertiginous austerity and quiet serenity. Through a process-driven approach, she experiments with the sensuousness of materials that register both touch and distance. Her practice holds onto what cannot be fully recalled, trusting that intimacy, care, and material presence can speak for the overlooked.
